# Cyberbullying victimization/perpetration — Russian CAV adaptation

- Status: `implemented-local`; not deployed.
- The backlog's English labels refer to two instrument constructs, not an identified common Russian form. Instead of claiming identity, the catalog adds the separately named Russian adaptation of the Cyber-Aggression and Cyber-Victimization scale (CAV) by Mikhailova & Istomina (2025).
- Russian sample: N=1020, ages 17–35, Russia.
- 24 Russian items, response frequency 0–4 (0 never to 4 constantly). The article only specifies endpoint labels; intermediate numeric choices remain numeric.
- Factor assignment: CAV items 1–12 are Cyber-Aggression; 13–24 are Cyber-Victimization. Each is summed independently, range 0–48. No severity thresholds are published.
- Reliability: alpha .839 and .847 respectively (article's summary orders victimization/aggression; its table reports victimization .847 and aggression .839). The code mapping was checked against the rotated factor table, where CAV_1–12 load on aggression and CAV_13–24 on victimization.
